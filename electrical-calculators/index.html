Electrical & Lighting Calculators

Electrical sizing is about heat and safety margins. These calculators estimate electrical load and breaker size, pick a wire gauge by ampacity, check conduit and cable tray fill, size a generator transfer switch, and lay out recessed lights. Each shows its method and assumptions. They are for planning and learning: electrical work must meet your local code and should be done or inspected by a licensed electrician.
