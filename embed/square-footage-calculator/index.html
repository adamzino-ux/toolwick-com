Why This Matters

Almost every home project starts with area. Flooring, paint, drywall, insulation, sod, concrete, roofing underlayment and carpet are all sold or priced by the square foot or something derived from it. A small mistake multiplies quickly: a 12 by 15 foot bedroom is 180 square feet, and if you misjudge one wall by just 6 inches you are off by about 7 square feet, enough to leave a visible gap in a floor install or an extra box short on tile.

Homeowners use square footage to compare quotes, since a contractor charging $6 per square foot on 400 square feet of hardwood is a $2,400 job before extras. Landlords and buyers use it to compare price per square foot between listings. Gardeners use it to size mulch, seed and fertilizer coverage, and painters use it to estimate coats. Because material is sold in whole boxes, rolls and sheets, the number you actually order is always a little larger than the raw area, which is why this tool adds a waste allowance and rounds the order quantity up rather than down.

How It's Calculated

Every dimension is converted to feet first, then the standard area formula for the shape is applied:

Rectangle: A = length × width
Triangle: A = ½ × base × height
Circle: A = π × r² (r = diameter ÷ 2)
Trapezoid: A = ½ × (a + b) × height

For multiple rooms, each room's area is calculated as length × width, then added or subtracted according to your selection. The conversions used are 1 sq yd = 9 sq ft, 1 sq m = 10.7639 sq ft (1 sq ft = 0.09290304 sq m), 1 sq ft = 144 sq in, and 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft.

The waste step multiplies the area by (1 + waste ÷ 100) and then rounds the result up to the next whole square foot, so you are never shorted. Cost is calculated on that rounded-up order quantity. Common rules of thumb are 5-10% waste for straight-lay flooring, 10-15% for tile or diagonal patterns, and more for intricate layouts, but your product's manufacturer guidance takes priority.

This tool measures geometric area. It is not an appraisal, permit or inspection document. Real-estate listings often follow a different method, such as ANSI Z765 for single-family homes, which measures finished above-grade space from the exterior walls, so a listing number can differ from your room-by-room flooring area. Local codes, appraisal practice and supplier rules vary, so always confirm quantities with your supplier or local authority.

Worked Example

Say you are installing laminate flooring in a living room that measures 18 feet by 14 feet, with 10% waste and a price of $4.50 per square foot.

  1. Area = 18 × 14 = 252.00 sq ft.
  2. Conversions: 252 ÷ 9 = 28.00 sq yd; 252 × 0.09290304 = 23.41 sq m; 252 × 144 = 36,288 sq in; 252 ÷ 43,560 = 0.0058 acres.
  3. Perimeter = 2 × (18 + 14) = 64 ft, handy for estimating baseboard.
  4. Waste: 252 × 1.10 = 277.20 sq ft, rounded up to an order quantity of 278 sq ft.
  5. Estimated cost: 278 × $4.50 = $1,251.00.

Tips & Common Mistakes

  • Don't mix units. Pick one unit for every dimension. If you measured 10 ft 6 in, enter 10.5 ft (divide inches by 12) or choose inches and enter 126.
  • Break odd shapes into pieces. An L-shaped room is two rectangles. A room with a bay window might be a rectangle plus a trapezoid. Calculate each and add them, or use the Multiple rooms tab.
  • Measure the true height of a triangle. Use the perpendicular height, not the slanted side, or the area will come out too large.
  • Roofs are not flat. A roof's surface area is bigger than the footprint beneath it because of the slope. Multiply the footprint by a pitch factor before ordering roofing material.
  • Always add waste. Cuts, damaged pieces and pattern matching consume material. Buying exactly the measured area usually means a second trip to the store, and dye lots may not match.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate square footage of a room?

Measure the length and width of the room in feet and multiply them together. A 12 ft by 15 ft room is 12 × 15 = 180 square feet. For an irregular room, split it into rectangles, calculate each, and add them.

How do I calculate square footage of an L-shaped room?

Divide the L into two rectangles, measure each, and add the areas. In this calculator, use the Multiple rooms tab and enter each rectangle as its own row. Add a third row set to "Subtract" if part of the space is a cutout.

How many square feet are in a square yard?

There are 9 square feet in one square yard, because a yard is 3 feet and 3 × 3 = 9. Carpet is often priced per square yard, so divide your square footage by 9 to compare prices. The results table shows this conversion automatically.

How much extra should I order for waste?

A common rule of thumb is 5-10% for straight flooring layouts and 10-15% for tile, diagonal patterns or rooms with many corners. Check the manufacturer's recommendation, since some products need more. This tool rounds the final order up to the next whole square foot.

Is the square footage in a listing the same as flooring area?

Not always. Many agents and appraisers follow standards like ANSI Z765, which measures finished above-grade space from exterior walls and includes wall thickness. Flooring is measured on the interior of each room, so your flooring area will usually be smaller. Local practice varies.

How do I convert square meters to square feet?

Multiply square meters by 10.7639 to get square feet. To go the other way, multiply square feet by 0.09290304. Choose meters as the unit in this calculator, and it converts for you.
