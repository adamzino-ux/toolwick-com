All tools on toolwick.com run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data you enter into our tools.

Your Privacy Choices (US state laws)

Residents of California and other US states with consumer privacy laws may opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Where required, a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" choice is shown on this site by Google's consent tool. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as an opt-out where applicable. You can also opt out of personalized Google ads at adssettings.google.com.

Visitors from the EU, EEA and UK

Visitors from these regions are asked for consent before advertising cookies are used, through Google's certified consent management tool. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link shown by that tool.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We never sell or share email addresses.

Children

We may use basic analytics to understand which tools are popular. No personal information is collected.
