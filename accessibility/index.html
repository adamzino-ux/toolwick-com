Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 28, 2026

We want every calculator on toolwick.com to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Calculators use labeled form fields, work with the keyboard, and do not rely on color alone to convey results. We review pages regularly and fix issues as we find them.

Known limitations

Some older calculators may still have issues such as low-contrast text or charts without a full text alternative. We are working through them.

Feedback

If something on this site is hard to use, please contact us and describe the page and the problem. We aim to respond within five business days and will provide the information in another format if we can.
