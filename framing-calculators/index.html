Framing & Structural Calculators

Framing numbers decide whether a floor feels solid and a stair passes inspection. These calculators size floor joists and header beams, space trusses, lay out stair stringers, set knee wall stud heights, and work out rough openings for windows, doors and pocket doors. They also count lag bolts for a deck ledger, total board feet, and turn a list of parts into a plywood cut plan. Each one states its assumptions. Treat the results as planning estimates, because your local building department has the final word on structural sizes.
